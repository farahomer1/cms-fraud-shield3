VA BENEFIT CODES REFERENCE

=== CLAIM TYPES ===
MEDICAL: Healthcare services (inpatient, outpatient, emergency)
DISABILITY: Service-connected disability compensation (rated 0-100%)
PENSION: Non-service-connected pension benefits (needs-based)
DENTAL: Dental care benefits

F03.90 - Unspecified dementia without behavioral disturbance
G30.9 - Alzheimer's disease, unspecified
M17.11 - Primary osteoarthritis, right knee
Z96.641 - Presence of right artificial knee joint
